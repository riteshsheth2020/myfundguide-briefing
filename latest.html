Methodology

Engine v1.1 (deterministic, stdlib Python). All scores, bands, states and views come from the engine; nothing is adjusted by hand. On weekends and exchange holidays the engine is not re-run and the last confirmed readings are shown.

Verdict: normalised ≥ +0.33 RISK-ON, ≤ −0.33 RISK-OFF; withheld below 60% coverage; Low confidence below 80% or when best/worst-case bounds disagree.

Valuation bands by expensiveness percentile: <10 Deep Value, 10–30 Value, 30–70 Fair, 70–90 Expensive, ≥90 Very Expensive; borderline within 3 pts; DY inverted. Baselines from niftyindices.com export: Nifty 50 P/E & DY 31 Mar 2021–5 Nov 2026 (1,389 obs), P/B 29 Sep 2023–5 Nov 2026 (769 obs); Midcap 150 and Smallcap 250 31 Mar 2021–5 Nov 2026 (1,389 obs).

Liquidity: LAF surplus (avg of up to 5 obs), net durable liquidity, WACR − repo (±5 bps); threshold 1% of NDTL or ₹1 lakh cr proxy; sum ≥2 Easy, ≤−2 Tight.

Confirmation: any change in verdict, band, state or category view must repeat on two consecutive runs. Material moves: risk score ±0.15, valuation percentile ±5 pts.

Last-close fallback: each input uses (1) verified — official or two agreeing sources; (2) single-source; (3) last close — the most recent sourced close, at most 5 sessions old for daily series, or the latest official release for weekly/monthly series. Values are never estimated or derived; "missing" is used only when none exists.
