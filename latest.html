Methodology

  • Engine v1.1, deterministic, stdlib Python. All scores, bands, states and views on this page come from the engine output; nothing is adjusted by hand.
  • Risk: 15 signals scored +1/0/−1 (thresholds in the scorecard). Normalised score = sum ÷ signals scored. RISK-ON ≥ +0.33, RISK-OFF ≤ −0.33; verdict withheld below 60% coverage; Low confidence below 80% or when the best/worst-case bounds give different verdicts.
  • Liquidity: LAF surplus (avg of last 5 obs), net durable liquidity and WACR − repo, each ±1 against ±1% of NDTL (₹1 lakh cr proxy when NDTL is unavailable) and ±5 bps. Sum ≥ +2 Easy, ≤ −2 Tight.
  • Valuation: percentile of today's value in NSE daily history (niftyindices export). Nifty 50 P/E & DY from 31 Mar 2021 (1,389 obs), P/B from 29 Sep 2023 (769 obs); Midcap 150 and Smallcap 250 from 31 Mar 2021. Composite = median band of classified metrics.
  • Cycles count only with ≥ 2 sourced evidence items. Category view = mean of its drivers; ≥ +0.34 Favourable, ≤ −0.34 Cautious; "Lean" when inputs disagree. Credit Risk capped at Neutral.
  • A change in any verdict, band, state or view is confirmed only when it repeats on two consecutive runs.
  • Last-close fallback: each input uses (1) verified — official or two agreeing sources; else (2) single source; else (3) the latest earlier sourced close, at most 5 sessions old (latest official release for weekly/monthly data). Values are never estimated, interpolated or derived from other values. Each value carries its basis tag.
