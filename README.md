# myfundguide-briefing

Daily files for https://myfundguide.com, written by the morning briefing task.

- latest.html: today's Morning Market Briefing (archive/ keeps each day)
- fund-extras.json: fund size and risk ratios for the funds on the site
- nfo.json: current new fund offers
- flows.json: AMFI monthly category flows
- fund-list.json: the funds the website tracks (AMFI Regular Growth codes)

For information and education only. Not investment advice. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully.
