Methodology

Deterministic engine v1.1. Data are gathered and labelled; the engine does all scoring. No score, band or view is adjusted by hand.

  • Risk: 15 signals, each +1/0/−1 (rules in the scorecard). Normalized = sum ÷ scored. RISK-ON ≥ +0.33, RISK-OFF ≤ −0.33. Verdict withheld below 60% coverage; confidence Low below 80% or if best/worst-case bounds disagree.
  • Liquidity: LAF surplus (5-obs average), net durable liquidity, WACR − repo (±5 bps). Threshold 1% of NDTL, else ₹1 lakh cr. Sum ≥2 Easy, ≤−2 Tight.
  • Valuation: percentile vs NSE baseline (≥750 obs, single methodology). Bands at 10/30/70/90; DY inverted. Nifty 50 P/E & DY baseline 31 Mar 2021–5 Nov 2026 (n=1,389); P/B 29 Sep 2023–5 Nov 2026 (n=769); Midcap 150 and Smallcap 250 31 Mar 2021–5 Nov 2026 (n=1,389). Source: niftyindices.com export.
  • Cycles: a phase counts only with ≥2 sourced evidence items.
  • Category Compass: mean of driver scores; ≥ +0.34 Favourable, ≤ −0.34 Cautious; Credit Risk capped at Neutral. Low confidence → "Lean". Every state change needs two consecutive runs.
  • Macro drivers: CPI −1 (6.09% > 4% and rising); curve 0 (10Y 7.265% − 364-day T-bill 6.6387% = 62.6 bps; +1 above 75, −1 below 25).
  • Last-close fallback: each input uses, in order, verified (official or two agreeing sources), single source, or the most recent sourced close within 5 sessions (latest release for weekly/monthly series). Values are never estimated, interpolated or rolled forward. 5-day changes use two sourced closes (27 Nov vs 19 Nov).
